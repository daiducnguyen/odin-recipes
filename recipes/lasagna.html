Lasagna

Home

Description

A classic lasagna recipe involves layering wide flat pasta sheets with a savory meat and tomato ragù, a creamy ricotta or béchamel cheese mixture, and plenty of shredded mozzarella and Parmesan, then baking the assembled dish in the oven until bubbly, golden brown, and set.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground meat
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Aromatics
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Prep the oven
  2. Brown the meat
  3. Simmer the sauce
  4. Boil the noodles
  5. Mix the cheese filling
  6. Spread the base layer
