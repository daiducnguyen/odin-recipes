Steak

Home

Description

A thick cut of beef seasoned with salt and pepper, seared at high heat for a deep crust, and basted with rich butter, garlic, and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • A thick-cut steak (at least 1.5 to 2 inches thick)
  • Coarse Salt
  • Fresh Black Pepper
  • High-Smoke-Point Oil
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Fresh Garlic
  • Hard Herbs: thyme or rosemary

Steps

  1. Prep the meat: Bring the steak to room temperature for 30 minutes and pat it completely dry with paper towels.
  2. Season heavily: Coat all sides of the steak generously with kosher salt and coarsely ground black pepper right before cooking.
  3. Heat the pan: Get a heavy cast-iron skillet smoking hot over high heat and add one tablespoon of high-smoke-point oil.
  4. Sear the steak: Lay the steak in the pan and cook for 2 minutes without moving it to build a deep, golden-brown crust.
  5. Flip and repeat: Turn the steak over to sear the second side for another 2 minutes.
  6. Add the aromatics: Turn the heat down to medium and toss in 2–3 tablespoons of unsalted butter, smashed garlic cloves, and fresh rosemary or thyme.
  7. Baste continuously: Tilt the pan slightly and use a large spoon to continuously scoop the melted, foaming butter over the top of the steak for 1 to 2 minutes.
  8. Check the temperature: Insert a meat thermometer into the thickest part to catch your target doneness (130°F/54°C for medium-rare).
  9. Rest the meat: Remove the steak from the pan and let it rest on a warm plate or cutting board for 5 to 8 minutes to lock in the juices.
